How do I get invoices from my current app?
  1. Export your invoice list as CSV from your accounting app, or download your spreadsheet as CSV.
  2. Include invoice number, customer, amount in INR, due date (YYYY-MM-DD), and status (unpaid, pending, overdue, paid, draft, void, or uncollectible).
  3. Link or upload the CSV here. Preview and confirm before your workspace changes.

Common headings such as Invoice Number, Customer, Amount, Due Date and Status are supported. This is a file connection; private account and Google Sheets live sync are not connected. You do not need to publish your financial data.
